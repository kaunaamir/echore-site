AI Automation

echore helps ambitious businesses take repetitive work off their teams’ hands with practical, AI-powered automation. From handling customer conversations to organising enquiries and streamlining everyday admin, we connect the tools businesses already use and turn scattered tasks into smoother, more reliable workflows.

Our automation services bring WhatsApp, Instagram, and Gmail together with intelligent workflows that can respond to common questions, capture and qualify leads, route enquiries, send follow-ups, organise inboxes, and keep teams moving. Every solution is shaped around the way a business actually works, with human handover where it matters and clear control over what gets automated.

Good automation should feel less like adding another tool and more like removing friction from the way a business runs. echore maps the repetitive steps behind customer enquiries and internal admin, then builds connected workflows around them. On WhatsApp and Instagram, AI-assisted agents can answer routine questions, collect useful details, qualify leads, and pass conversations to a person whenever judgement or a personal touch is needed.

In Gmail, automations can sort incoming messages, identify priority enquiries, prepare draft replies, extract key information, and trigger timely follow-ups. These workflows can connect with calendars, spreadsheets, CRMs, and other business systems to reduce manual copying and missed handovers. We focus on practical outcomes: faster responses, more consistent processes, and more time for your team to do work that actually needs them.
